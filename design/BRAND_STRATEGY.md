# Sale Lagi Hai! Brand strategy

Working name: **Sale Lagi Hai!** / **سیل لگی ہے!** ("a sale is on!"). The identity is built so a rename only
changes the wordmark; the symbol, colors, type and voice survive (see "If the name changes" at the end).

## 1. Positioning

**For** urban Pakistanis aged 18–40 who love a good price and hate finding out about a sale after it ended,
**Sale Lagi Hai!** is the deals app that **tells you the moment a sale goes live**: clothing brands, food,
bank-card discounts, telecom bundles, groceries and salons, starting in Karachi and nationwide for brand and bank deals.
**Unlike** discount-card and coupon-book apps (pay first, then hunt through stale offers) and marketplaces (only
their own stock), we are free, fast, verified and made to be forwarded on WhatsApp.

**For businesses:** the place where people who are already looking for a deal find yours, with honest numbers
on who saw it.

**Promise:** *Sale ki khabar, sab se pehle.* / سیل کی خبر، سب سے پہلے۔ / "First to know when the sale is on."

## 2. Personality (5 traits)

| Trait | We are | We are not |
|---|---|---|
| **Bazaar-smart** | We know prices, know the season (lawn launch, 11.11, Eid, Black Friday), know which "70% off" is real. | Know-it-all, cynical, sarcastic about brands that pay us. |
| **Quick** | First with the news. Short sentences. The key fact (brand, %, until when) comes first. | Breathless, spammy, "HURRY!!!". |
| **Honest** | Exact numbers, real end dates, "up to" only when we say on what. Sponsored is labelled. | Clickbait, fake urgency, hidden conditions. |
| **Yaar (warm)** | The friend who texts you "sale lagi hai, chalo". Code-switches naturally. | Over-familiar, slangy to the point of cringe, meme-dependent. |
| **Proudly local** | Karachi first, Urdu treated with respect (real Nastaliq, correct spelling), local references done with taste. | Kitsch truck-art pastiche, flag-waving, "desi" as a costume. |

## 3. The big idea: *the shout, written properly*

"Sale lagi hai!" is not a slogan, it is something people **shout**: across the mall, in the family WhatsApp group,
to the friend who has been waiting for Sapphire's sale. Our whole brand is that shout. The exclamation mark is the
hero of the identity.

But a shout from a deals app is only worth listening to if it is **trustworthy**. So the shout is written with the
discipline of a calligrapher: our exclamation mark is drawn the way a *khattat* writes with a reed pen (*qalam*).
The bar is a Nastaliq **alif**: cut flat at the nib's angle, full width, easing to a point as the pen lifts.
The dot is a **nuqta**, the diamond the same nib stamps, the unit calligraphers have used for a thousand years to
measure every letter. To an English reader it is a bold "!"; to an Urdu reader it carries the hand of the script they
grew up with. The same mark closes both "Sale Lagi Hai!" and "سیل لگی ہے!".

In one line: **Loud, but well-made.** *Shor, magar saleeqe se.* (شور، مگر سلیقے سے)

How the idea runs through the system:
- **The Qalam Bang** is the app icon, the notification icon, the splash, and the end of the wordmark.
- **The nuqta** is a brand device: it dots every "i" in the wordmark, marks bullets and "new" states, and is the
  "stamp" motion when a new deal lands.
- **Haldi (turmeric) yellow + Surma (kohl) ink**: the colors of a hand-painted bazaar board, confident and legible
  from across the street. **Mirch (chilli) red** appears only on discount badges, so red always means "money off".
- **One exclamation mark per message, and it's ours.** The brand owns the "!"; copy never stacks them.

## 4. Voice and tone

Voice stays the same; tone flexes by moment.

| Moment | Tone | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| New sale / alert | Excited, factual | Lead with brand + offer + deadline. One "!". One emoji max (🔔 for alerts only). |
| Browsing / empty | Easy, friendly | Say what's happening and what to do. A little bazaar warmth is OK. |
| Errors | Calm, helpful | Say what happened, what we're doing, what they can do. Never blame. No jokes. |
| Money / cards / business | Precise, formal-polite | Exact terms, conditions upfront. Urdu uses "آپ". |
| Business owners | Professional, partner-like | Numbers, outcomes, clarity on what "Featured" means. |

**Language:** English UI with Urdu fully supported. Roman Urdu is for marketing, push and social where our users
naturally mix ("Sale lagi hai, 40% off!"); the Urdu locale uses proper Urdu script, never Roman Urdu.
Brand names stay in their own spelling (Sapphire, Khaadi) in every language.

**Words we like:** live, shuru, ends Sunday, verified, near you, flat 40%, card deal.
**Words we avoid:** "up to 70%" without saying on what, "hurry", "last chance" (unless true), "best ever", "mega".

## 5. If the name changes

- Keep: the Qalam Bang symbol, the nuqta device, palette, type, voice, icon set (none contain the name).
- Redo: the English wordmark (regenerate with `design/_src/brand.py` → `wordmark_paths(text=...)`), the Urdu
  lockup (`urdu_paths`), lockups and the board. Each is a script run, not a redraw.
- The idea needs the new name to end in, or be said as, an exclamation. Most Urdu/Roman-Urdu deal names do
  ("Sasta Hai!", "Lag Gayi!"). A name without that energy would weaken the big idea.
