The idea

The shout, written properly.

"Sale lagi hai!" is something people shout. Our exclamation mark is written the way a calligrapher writes with a reed pen: the bar is a Nastaliq alif, the dot is a nuqta, the unit of calligraphic proportion.

A bold "!" to everyone; the hand of the Urdu script to Urdu readers. Loud, but well-made.
