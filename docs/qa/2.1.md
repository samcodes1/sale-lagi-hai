## What held up
- **Status graph** (`deals.sql:133-164`): draft→expired, published→pending_review and published→rejected are refused, and an ended deal can't be published. Publishing stamps `published_at`.
- **Deferred rules** (`deals.sql:218-276`): a local deal and its target can be written in one statement. A published local deal with no target fails at commit (`23514`).
- **Area/city consistency**: a target or outlet that pairs a Lahore area with Karachi gets `23503` (composite FK).
- **Restrict vs cascade**: deleting a city with areas, a category in use, a bank with products, or a business with payments is refused. Account deletion cascades as documented (see 2.2).

Suggested fixes:
- **C1:** Generalise the check. For `flat_percent_off`, use the same equality as `percent_off`. For `up_to_percent_off`, either forbid `price_pkr`/`original_price_pkr` (a range has no single price pair) or require `percent >= computed`. For `amount_off`, require `original_price_pkr - price_pkr = amount_off_pkr` when both prices are set, and `amount_off_pkr < original_price_pkr`. Alternatively forbid prices on every kind except `percent_off` and `fixed_price`, which matches "each offer kind has exactly the fields it needs" (SCHEMA.md).
- **C2:** Compute in bigint: `((original_price_pkr - price_pkr)::bigint * 100) / original_price_pkr`.
- **C3:** Restamp when publishing from `draft`/`removed`/`expired` (`new.published_at := now()`), or document that position is kept.
- **C4:** In `check_deal_status`, reject inserts whose status isn't draft, pending_review or published.
