### BUG-1 (S2): Signed-in follows never reach the account
- **Where:** `lib/features/follows/application/follow_providers.dart:48`.
- **Cause:** `insert('follows', {column: id})` sends no `user_id`, while `follows.user_id` is `not null` with no default and the policy checks `user_id = auth.uid()`.
  - In Postgres as a shopper: `42501: new row violates row-level security policy for table "follows"` (QA SQL TODO test).
  - The app logs "Could not sync a follow" and keeps the phone copy.
- **Effect:**
  - Unfollow does reach the server (the delete sends `user_id`), so the account and the phone drift apart.
  - Follows feed the alerts (Phase 5), so a shopper who signs in and then follows brands gets none of their alerts.
  - EXPLORE.md's fallback ("the guest sync copies it next time") only runs at the next sign-in.
- **Why the tests missed it:** the developer's "signed in, follows also change in the account" passes only because `DemoGateway` accepts any row.
- **Reproducers:** `explore_qa_test.dart`, "signed in, following a brand / a category reaches the account" (`RulesGateway`).
- **Suggested fix:** as 4.4 BUG-1: send `'user_id': user.id`, or add `default auth.uid()` to `follows.user_id` in a new migration.

### BUG-2 (S3): Explore shows at most 60 brands
- **Where:**
  - `lib/features/catalog/data/backend_catalog_repository.dart:79` (`limit = 60`) and `:88`;
  - `explore_screen.dart` (`brandsForInterestsProvider('')`).
- **Cause:** Explore asks once for the active brands, by name, 60 at most, and has no paging. EXPLORE.md says "every brand".
- **Effect:**
  - 17 brands are seeded today, so it's not visible yet.
  - With more than 60, every brand after the 60th by name (most of "N" to "Z") can't be found from Explore. Search (4.6) and deal cards would be the only ways in.
- **Reproducer:** "every brand can be found, also beyond the first 60" (70 brands; "QA Brand 69" never appears).
- **Suggested fix:** page the list (keyset by name), or group brands by category behind the chips.

### BUG-3 (S3): The category app bar at 200%
- **Where:** `lib/features/categories/presentation/category_screen.dart:82–91`. The Follow button sits in `AppBar.actions`.
- **Steps:** open `/category/food-and-restaurants` at 200% text on a 360-wide phone.
- **Actual:**
  - **English:** the title shrinks to "Food …" beside a large "+ Follow".
  - **Urdu:** the title is "کھانا او…", and the Follow label "فالو کریں" is **cut off top and bottom** by the toolbar's fixed height, so it's hardly readable (`screens/4.5/web/category_360x800_ur-PK_light_ts2_p0.png`).
  - The page has no other heading, so the category name is effectively gone.
  - In widget tests (test font), the Urdu title gets 0 px.
- **Expected:** the name and Follow both readable, as on the brand page, which puts Follow in the body.
- **Reproducer:** "ur 200% on 360 × 800: the category name stays readable beside Follow in the app bar".
- **Suggested fix:** move Follow into the page body under a heading (like `_Page` on the brand screen), or into an overflow at large text.

### BUG-4 (S4): "Visit website" gives no feedback when it can't open
- **Where:** `lib/features/brands/presentation/brand_screen.dart:146–152`.
- **Cause:** the result of `openWeb` is ignored. The `https_url` domain allows URLs that `LinkService` refuses, such as `https://user@site.pk` (userinfo), and a device may have no browser.
- **Expected:** "Couldn't open that", as on the deal page.
- **Reproducer:** "a brand website that cannot open says so".

### BUG-5 (S4): A quick double tap on Follow shows the wrong note
- **Where:** `lib/features/follows/presentation/follow_button.dart:34–38`.
- **Cause:** `following` and the note are fixed when the button builds, and there's no guard against re-entry. Two taps in one frame toggle twice (follow, then unfollow), and both show "Following X…" while the brand ends up not followed.
- **Reproducer:** "a quick double tap: the note matches what happened".
- **Suggested fix:** ignore taps while a toggle runs, or build the note from the state after the toggle.

- **Lists kept for the app's lifetime:** `dealListProvider` is a family without `autoDispose`, so every category or brand list visited stays in memory, with all its pages.
