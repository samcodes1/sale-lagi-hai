## What held up
- **No recursion:** every role reads every table and view (test 1).
- **Anon's surface:** anon can select exactly 12 relations (test 2), and has no write grants (lint).
- **RETURNING:** `update/delete … returning` on hidden rows returns nothing (tests 3 and 4).
- **Token hook:** it replaces an injected `user_roles` claim. Only `supabase_auth_admin` can execute it.
- **Definer helpers:**
  - `search_hits`, `outlets_near`, `follows_deal`, `recent_save_counts`, `can_write_media`, `my_recent_uploads` and `register_push_token` all pin `search_path=''` and filter by `auth.uid()` or public status.
  - `search_hits` never returns drafts (test 6).
  - `private` isn't exposed (`config.toml` `schemas = ["public"]`).
- **IDOR:**
  - A member can't move a draft to another business's brand, and can't touch another business's deals (0 rows).
  - A placement for another business's deal is refused by `check_placement_deal`.
  - Saving or reporting a draft by id gives `42501`, the same as for a non-existent id, so there is no existence oracle.
- **Escalation:** shoppers can't self-grant roles. Owners can't verify themselves. Members can't publish, verify, activate or change `bank_id`. An admin can't drop their own admin role.
- **Account deletion** (tests 17–18): profiles, alert_prefs, follows, saves, user_cards, notifications, push_tokens, push_outbox, roles and memberships are all deleted. Reports and submissions are kept with a null id. A sole owner's account deletion succeeds and leaves the business ownerless, as documented.

## Recommendations (non-blocking)
- `businesses: register` has no limit. One account can create unlimited pending businesses. Add a per-user cap in a trigger, as for submissions.
- An owner adding arbitrary users also lets anyone see a stranger's uuid show up in their team. Use invitations by phone or email in the portal flow.
- Moderators can verify a business they belong to. Consider forbidding self-verification.
- Placements keep promoting a deal after its brand changes owner: `check_placement_deal` only fires on `deal_id`/`business_id` changes.
