### BUG-1 (S2): One account's data goes into another's on a shared phone
- **Where:**
  - `lib/features/auth/application/auth_providers.dart:66` and `:83`: the guest sync inserts every saved deal and card on the phone into the account signing in.
  - `:125–129`: since 4.8, it first pulls the account's saves, cards and follows onto the phone.
  - Sign-out (Profile) keeps everything on the phone: "The on-phone choices stay" (ACCOUNTS.md).
- **Steps:**
  1. A signs in on a family phone. A's saves, cards and follows from A's other phones come onto it.
  2. A signs out.
  3. B signs in on the same phone.
- **Expected:** B's account gets only what B chose.
- **Actual:** B's account now holds A's saved deals and A's **cards**, meaning which banks' cards A has, and the phone shows A's follows as B's.
  - That's personal and financial-adjacent data moving between accounts with nobody asking.
  - It persists on the server, and from there reaches every phone B signs in on.
- **Reproducers:** `inbox_qa_test.dart`, "a shared phone: what account A brought onto it does not go into account B…" and "a shared phone: A's cards are not added to B's account".
- **Suggested fix:** pick one.
  - On sign-out, remove from the phone what the account brought (keep the origin of each entry).
  - Or ask "Keep your saved deals on this phone?" at sign-out.
  - Or upload at sign-in only what was added while a guest.

### BUG-2 (S3): Removed deals come back
- **Where:** `lib/features/saved/presentation/saved_screen.dart:54–61`. `_removeAll` (used by "Remove ended deals" and "Remove them") changes the phone list with `update` directly; unlike `toggle`, it never deletes from `saves`.
- **Steps:**
  1. Signed in, a saved deal ends.
  2. Tap Ended, then "Remove ended deals".
- **Expected:** the deal is gone from the account too.
- **Actual:** it's still in `saves`, so the next sign-in pull (this phone or another) brings it back.
- **Reproducer:** "Remove ended deals also removes them from the account".
- **Suggested fix:** a `SavedDeals.removeAll(ids)` that mirrors the deletes (one `delete … in (ids)` request).

### BUG-3 (S3): Sign-in waits for hundreds of requests
- **Where:** `auth_providers.dart:66–96`. Each saved deal (up to 500) and each card is inserted one request at a time, awaited in turn. An already-synced one fails as a duplicate and is counted. The sign-in screen awaits the whole sync (`_signedIn`) before it closes.
- **Effect:** with 500 saves, 500 round trips. On a 150 ms mobile link, that's over a minute on a spinner, and it repeats at every sign-in, because duplicates are re-sent.
- **Reproducer:** "signing in with 500 saved deals does not wait for 500 requests" counts 500 inserts.
- **Suggested fix:** one bulk insert per table, with `onConflict` / `ignoreDuplicates` (PostgREST upsert), or an RPC; and don't block the sign-in screen on it.

### BUG-4 (S4): A read alert turns unread again
- **Where:** `lib/features/alerts/application/alerts_providers.dart:97–122`. `loadMore` builds the new state from the `current` it read before the request, so a `markRead` made meanwhile is overwritten when the page lands.
- **Effect:** the server has it read; the next load shows it right.
- **Reproducer:** "an alert read while the next page is loading stays read".
- **Suggested fix:** build the result from `state.value` after the await, or apply pending reads on top.

### BUG-5 (S4): Opening the Alerts tab again doesn't clear the badge
- **Where:** `lib/features/alerts/presentation/alerts_screen.dart:34`. `markAlertsSeen` runs in `initState`, but the tab shell keeps each tab's state, so it runs only on the first visit.
- **Steps:** open Alerts, go Home, new alerts come in (badge "2"), open Alerts again.
- **Actual:** the badge stays.
- **Severity:** latent today, because nothing refreshes the badge mid-session. It will show as soon as alerts arrive live (Phase 5 push).
- **Reproducer:** "re-opening the Alerts tab clears a badge that came since".
- **Suggested fix:** mark seen when the tab is selected (shell `onDestinationSelected`, or a route listener).

### BUG-6 (S4): Undo doesn't put the deal back in place
- **Where:** `saved_screen.dart:47`. Undo calls `toggle`, which saves the deal as the newest.
- **Steps:** unsave the second of three saved deals, then Undo.
- **Expected:** the order [1, 2, 3].
- **Actual:** [2, 1, 3].
- **Reproducer:** "Undo puts a deal back where it was".
- **Suggested fix:** remember the index and re-insert there.

### BUG-7 (S4): Switched-off follows can't be unfollowed
- **Where:**
  - `followedBrandsProvider` → `brandsByIds` reads only active brands (`backend_catalog_repository.dart:175`);
  - `FollowingScreen` lists only those, and active categories;
  - the Alerts "Following" summary counts every id (`alerts_screen.dart:228`).
- **Steps:** follow two brands; one is switched off.
- **Actual:** the summary says "2 brands" but the list shows 1, and the other can never be unfollowed. It also stays in `follows`.
- **Reproducer:** "a followed brand that has gone can still be unfollowed, and the count matches the list".
- **Suggested fix:** count what is listed, and show a "no longer available: Remove" row for the rest, as Saved does for gone deals.

### BUG-8 (S4): The badge isn't heard as designed
- **Where:** `lib/app/shell/app_shell.dart:110`. The badge's text is merged into the tab's label, and the `tooltip` with "Alerts, 2 new alerts" never reaches the semantics tree.
- **Effect:** TalkBack reads "2 Alerts" or "99+ Alerts". The number is there, but not what it means, and SAVED_AND_ALERTS.md promises "Alerts, 2 new alerts".
- **Reproducer:** "the badge is heard as 'Alerts, N new alerts'…". No semantics node says "new alert".
- **Suggested fix:**
  - wrap the icon in `Semantics(label: …, excludeSemantics: true)`, or exclude the badge and set the destination's label;
  - keep "99+" (or "more than 99") for the capped count rather than "100".

## Notes (not filed)
- **Unstable alert order:** the inbox pages by `offset` and orders by `created_at` only (`alerts_providers.dart:73`). Alerts from one dispatcher run can share a `created_at`, and ties across a page boundary have no stable order, so one could repeat (hidden by de-duplication) and another be skipped. Order by `created_at desc, id desc`, and consider a keyset.
- **Badge clock:** the badge's `since` is the phone's clock, compared with server times. A phone clock that's ahead hides new alerts from the badge until it catches up.
- **Seen time per phone:** `alerts.seen_at.v1` is per phone, not per account, so a second account on the phone starts from the first one's time.
- **Cold start:** `inboxProvider` and the badge are first built before `currentUserProvider` has a value, so for a frame a signed-in shopper is treated as a guest. If Alerts is the first screen, the guest prompt flashes.
- **Saved at startup:** Saved shows "no saved deals" until the saved file has been read.
- **Following refetches:** unfollowing re-reads the followed brands, and the list flashes its skeleton.
- **Wording:** with one gone deal: "1 saved deal is no longer available." followed by **Remove them**.

#### BUG-9 (S3): After sign-out, the account's lists stay on the phone, and the next guest's saves are later dropped
- **Where:**
  - Sign-out never clears the phone's lists or `account.on_phone.v1`:
    - `lib/features/profile/presentation/profile_screen.dart:140`;
    - `lib/features/settings/presentation/privacy_screen.dart:48`;
    - `lib/features/auth/presentation/sign_in_screen.dart:197` (wrong account on re-auth).
  - At the next sign-in by another account, `lib/features/auth/application/auth_providers.dart:65–69` drops everything on the phone.
- **Steps:**
  1. A signs in on a shared phone. A's saves and cards from A's other phones come onto it (the 4.8 pull).
  2. A signs out.
  3. B opens Saved and My Cards as a guest.
  4. B saves a deal and adds a card, then signs in.
- **Expected:**
  - After A signs out, the phone no longer shows A's saved deals or which bank cards A holds.
  - B's guest saves and card reach B's account.
- **Actual:**
  - After step 2, A's saves and cards, including those from A's other phones, are still shown to whoever uses the phone.
  - At step 4, B's guest saves and card are dropped from the phone without a word, and never reach B's account.
- **Notes:**
  - The second half is the trade-off documented in SAVED_AND_ALERTS.md. It is new in round 2: before the fix, they were uploaded, along with A's.
  - The first half was in round 1's BUG-1 description ("Sign-out keeps everything on the phone"), but not in its expected result.
  - Workaround: remove the items by hand, or save them again after signing in.
- **Reproducers:** `test/qa/inbox_qa_test.dart`, group "round 2: sign-in":
  - "BUG-9: after signing out, the account's saved deals and cards no longer show on the phone";
  - "BUG-9: what a guest saves after another account signed out goes into their account when they sign in".
  - Both are skipped unless `QA_KNOWN_BUGS=true`.
- **Suggested fix:** at sign-out, clear the saved deals, cards and follows the account brought, or all of them, and remove the note. A guest after sign-out then starts clean, and their saves sync normally. The trade-off is no longer needed.

### Notes (not filed)
- **Unreadable note:** if `account.on_phone.v1` can't be read, a first-time guest's saves, cards and follows are dropped as if they were someone else's. This is documented and rare: the same store just read the lists.
- **City and language:** `sync_guest_preferences` still sends the phone's city and language into whichever account signs in, overwriting that account's home city. Since the phone is now that person's, this is arguably right. I'm recording it as a choice.
- **One bad id blocks the sync:** a malformed id in the saved list (e.g. a corrupted file) fails the whole `sync_guest_items` call (22P02). It is then retried, and fails, at every sign-in. Filtering to uuids on the client would contain it.
- **Remove all:** `removeAll` sends one `delete` per deal in parallel, up to 500 requests. One `delete … in (…)` would do it in one.
- **Offline after a switch:** when another account signs in offline, the phone is empty. That account's own saves come only at its next sign-in, because the pull runs only at sign-in.
- **`markAlertsSeen`** uses the shell's `WidgetRef` after an `await` (`alerts_providers.dart:198–229`). If the shell is disposed while the request is in flight, the unawaited call throws into the zone. It's unlikely, since the shell lives across tabs.
- **Undo on another phone:** Undo re-inserts the save with a new `created_at`. On the next sign-in elsewhere, that deal sorts to the top.
- **Demo only:**
  - `DemoGateway`'s `sync_guest_items` and `sync_guest_preferences` write as one fixed user (`demo_gateway.dart:299–345`).
  - `DemoAuthRepository` gives the same number a new id after sign-out. So in the web demo, signing out and back in counts as another account, and guest saves made in between are dropped.
