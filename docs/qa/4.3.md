### BUG-7 (S2): Screen readers can't activate the Home city button
- **Where:** `lib/features/home/presentation/home_screen.dart:94–98`.
- **Cause:** `_CityButton` wraps its `TextButton` in `Semantics(label:, hint:, button: true, excludeSemantics: true)` with no `onTap`. `excludeSemantics` drops the TextButton's own tap action, so the merged node is announced as a button but has **no tap action**.
  - `DealCard` handles exactly this, with a comment ("ExcludeSemantics below drops the InkWell's own tap action, so the merged node needs it") and `onTap:`.
  - Nowhere else lets the shopper change city after onboarding: `setCity` is only called here and in the onboarding city step.
- **Steps:**
  1. Turn TalkBack on and open Home.
  2. Focus "Deals in Karachi, Change city" and double-tap.
- **Expected:** the city sheet opens.
- **Actual:**
  - Nothing happens. TalkBack's double tap sends ACTION_CLICK, which Flutter maps to `SemanticsAction.tap`, and the node has no handler.
  - On the web, the DOM node has no `role="button"` and no click action, so browser screen readers can't activate it either.
  - A mouse or finger still works (checked on the web build). iOS VoiceOver may fall back to a touch at the activation point, but Android is the target.
- **Reproducer:** `home_qa_test.dart`, "screen reader › en/ur: a screen reader can activate the city button…". `hasAction(SemanticsAction.tap)` is false in both languages.
- **Suggested fix:** add `onTap: () => _pickCity(context, ref, urdu: urdu)` to that `Semantics`, as `DealCard` does. Extend `home_a11y_test.dart` to perform the tap.

### BUG-1 (S3): A stale "For you" page brings the old city's list back
- **Where:** `lib/features/home/application/home_providers.dart:229–231`.
- **Cause:** the stale-page check compares `state.value?.next` with the cursor the page was asked for. While the provider rebuilds for a new city or new interests, `state` is `AsyncLoading` and still carries the **previous** value, so `now.next == next` and the old page is accepted. `state = AsyncData(...)` then replaces the loading state with the old city's list.
- **Steps:**
  1. Scroll "For you" near the end, so the next page is in flight on a slow network.
  2. Before it lands, pick another city (or change interests).
- **Expected:** the new city's skeletons, then its deals.
- **Actual:** under the new city's name, "For you" shows the **old** city's 40 deals (the first page plus the stale one) until the new city's first page arrives. Recorded states: `[karachi x40, lahore x20]`. The final state is correct.
- **Reproducer:** `home_providers_qa_test.dart`, "the old page answers while the new city is still loading…". QA's other three race tests pass:
  - the new city answers first;
  - an interest change;
  - 50 calls while a page is in flight make one request.
- **Suggested fix:** drop the page when the provider rebuilt meanwhile, either with a generation counter bumped in `build()`, or by checking `state.isLoading` or `ref.mounted` after the await.

### BUG-2 (S3): The offline copy of "For you" shows deals that have ended
- **Where:** `lib/features/home/application/home_providers.dart:203–210`.
- **Cause:** the three carousels pass their copy through `_onlyLive`. `ForYouController.build` returns the cached rows as they are.
- **Steps:**
  1. Load Home while online, with a deal ending in an hour.
  2. Two hours later, open the app offline.
- **Expected:** the ended deal is left out ("Deals that ended since they were saved are dropped when shown", HOME.md).
- **Actual:** it shows in "For you", with an "Ended" time label, while the carousels drop it.
- **Reproducer:** `home_providers_qa_test.dart`, "For you: deals that ended since the copy was saved are left out…".
- **Suggested fix:** `deals: _live(deals, ref.read(clockProvider)())`.

### BUG-3 (S4): Offline "For you" claims the end of the list
- **Where:** `home_providers.dart:210` returns a cached `FeedState` with `next == null`, and `home_screen.dart:554` shows `homeFeedEnd` whenever `atEnd`.
- **Steps:**
  1. Open Home offline with a saved copy.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of "For you".
- **Expected:** a note that more loads when back online, or nothing.
- **Actual:** "You've seen all the new deals. Check back soon." after the 20 saved deals.
- **Reproducer:** `home_qa_test.dart`, "a saved first page does not claim to be the end of the list".
- **Suggested fix:** in `_FeedFooter`, show nothing (or an offline hint) when `state.cachedAt != null`.

### BUG-4 (S4): The home cache only grows, and interest order splits it
- **Where:** `home_providers.dart:184` and `json_cache.dart:91–94`.
- **Cause:**
  - The key is `home.foryou.<city>.<interestIds.join(',').hashCode>`, and `interestIds` keeps the order of toggling. `[food, fashion]` and `[fashion, food]` are two copies.
  - `JsonCache.get` ignores a copy past `maxAge` but never deletes it, and nothing else removes `cache.home.*`.
- **Size:**
  - The cache holds 4 rows per city (featured, trending, ending, For you), for 10 choices (All Pakistan + 9 cities), plus one For you copy per interest combination seen per city.
  - A card is about **1 KB** in storage (the demo copy is 9,825 characters for 10 cards). So a full 20-card row is about 20 KB, one city about 80 KB, and all cities about 0.8 MB.
  - Every interest combination adds about 20 KB per city, forever.
  - On the web (5 MB localStorage), writes start failing after a few hundred copies. The failure is caught and logged, so there's no crash.
  - On Android, `SharedPreferencesAsync` (DataStore) rewrites the whole file on each write, and Home writes 4 copies per load.
- **Expected:** one copy per city and interest set, and old copies removed.
- **Reproducers:** `home_providers_qa_test.dart`, "the same interests picked in another order share one copy" and "copies past maxAge are removed…".
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Key on the sorted ids (e.g. `(ids.toList()..sort()).join(',')`), not `hashCode`, which can also collide.
  - Delete expired entries in `get`.
  - Optionally keep only the last N For you copies.

### BUG-5 (S4): 975–999 m shows as "1,000 m"
- **Where:** `lib/core/l10n/formatters.dart:43–45`.
- **Cause:** metres under 1000 are rounded to 50 *after* the < 1000 check, so 975 rounds to 1000 and renders "1,000 m".
- **Expected:** "1 km".
- **Reproducer:** `home_qa_test.dart`, "975 m shows as 1 km…". QA's 11 boundary cases, the Urdu units and the Urdu digits all pass.
- **Suggested fix:** round first, then branch (`if (rounded < 1000)`).

### BUG-6 (S4): "Near you" results outlive account deletion
- **Where:** `nearMeProvider` (`home_providers.dart:290–296`) isn't reset by `_forgetThisPhone` (`delete_account_screen.dart:99`).
- **Steps:**
  1. Tap "Show deals near me".
  2. Delete the account and onboard again.
  3. Scroll to "Near you".
- **Expected:** the button again ("Local data goes too").
- **Actual:** the deleted account's nearby results, with their distances. The data is in memory only and is gone after a restart.
- **Reproducer:** `home_qa_test.dart`, "after the account is deleted (onboarding reset), Near you no longer shows the earlier results".
- **Suggested fix:** invalidate `nearMeProvider` (and the home rows) in `_forgetThisPhone`, or have `nearMeProvider` watch something that `reset()` changes.

## Performance
- **Web bundle:**
  - `main.dart.js` is 3,829,320 B raw and 1,102,021 B gzip -9.
  - Against `e6759a9` (4.1 + 4.2): **+69 KB raw (+1.8%), +19.6 KB gzip (+1.8%)**.
  - Web `assets/` grew by +208 B.
  - No new runtime fetch from 4.3.
- **Prefetch `addPostFrameCallback`:**
  - Each build of one of the last 5 items schedules a `loadMore`. `loadMore` returns at once while a page is loading, at the end, or after a failure (the builder also checks those).
  - So each page changes state twice, with no storm. The fling test settles with nothing scheduled.
  - **Note:** `_ForYou` maps **every** loaded deal to a `DealCard` on each rebuild (`_cards`, `home_screen.dart:457`), not just the visible ones. That's O(n) per page, and O(n²) over a long session. Mapping inside `itemBuilder` avoids it.
- **`ending_soon_deals` on 200k deals** (4,624 ending within 72 h; 1 in 3 local; inactive brands and categories), as anon, warm, JIT off:
  | Call | Time |
  |---|---|
  | `ending_soon_deals(null)` | 5.7 ms |
  | `ending_soon_deals(<city>)` | 31.6 ms |
  | `ending_soon_deals(<city>, 168, 50)` | 50.3 ms |
  | `feed(<city>)` (for comparison) | 3.1 ms |

  The plan is a bitmap scan of every deal in the window, then a top-N sort. That's fine at this size. An index on `(ends_at, id) where status = 'published'` would make it an ordered scan that stops early.
- **JIT:** with Postgres's default `jit = on`, each of these RPCs (and `feed`, `trending_deals`) takes **0.7–1.2 s**, almost all of it JIT compilation, because the inlined plans cross `jit_above_cost`. Check the hosted project (`show jit;`) and turn JIT off if it's on.
