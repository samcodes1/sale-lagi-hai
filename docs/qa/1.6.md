### BUG-1 (S2): Deal cards can't be activated by screen readers
- **Area / file:** `lib/design_system/widgets/deal_card.dart:66-77`. `Semantics(container: true, button: true, label: summary, customSemanticsActions: …)` wraps `ExcludeSemantics(child: InkWell(onTap: onTap, …))`. `ExcludeSemantics` drops the InkWell's tap action, and the `Semantics` node doesn't add `onTap`.
- **Environment:** every size (large, standard, compact), every locale and theme; widget test with `ensureSemantics`.
- **Steps:** Pump `DealCard(onTap: …)` and read `tester.getSemantics(find.bySemanticsLabel(RegExp('^Sapphire')))`.
- **Expected:** `isButton: true, hasTapAction: true`, and `SemanticsAction.tap` opens the deal (qa-testing §4; flutter-ui-ux: "The whole card is one tap target").
- **Actual:** `missing actions: [tap]`. The node announces "button" but has only the custom "Save" action. `performAction(tap)` does nothing.
  - TalkBack may fall back to injecting a touch at the node centre.
  - Switch Access, Voice Access ("tap Sapphire"), keyboard activation and Flutter web's DOM semantics have no such fallback.
- **Evidence:** `test/qa/design_system_qa_test.dart` "<size>: the card node can be activated (tap action)" fails ×3 (`Which: missing actions: [tap]`).
- **Severity rationale:** S3 on its own (there's a fallback on some screen readers). Escalated to **S2** under qa-testing §7: it breaks the core "open a deal" flow for assistive-technology users.
- **Suggested fix:** add `onTap: onTap` to the outer `Semantics` (line 66).
- **Regression test:** the QA test above.

### BUG-2 (S3): A free item breaks `DealOffer.fromPrices`
- **Area / file:** `lib/design_system/models/deal_offer.dart:15-17`, with the assertion at `:23-24`.
- **Steps:** `DealOffer.fromPrices(was: 500, now: 0)`.
- **Expected:** no crash. Return `null` (show the price "Rs 0"/"Free" as offer text), or cap at 99.
- **Actual:** the percent computes to 100. Debug builds throw `AssertionError: percent must be 1-99`, which crashes the card build or the mapper. Release builds produce `PercentOff(100)` and a "-100%" badge, the kind of claim deal-patterns §2 says never to inflate.
- **Evidence:** `test/qa/design_system_qa_test.dart` "a free item (now = 0) does not trip the 1-99 assertion" fails.
- **Suggested fix:** `if (was <= 0 || now <= 0 || now >= was) return null;`, or clamp to 99. Add the case to the existing "no real reduction" test.

### BUG-3 (S3): `AppStepper` step numbers clipped at large text
- **Area / file:** `lib/design_system/widgets/app_stepper.dart:56-62`. `Text(format.number(index + 1), style: TextStyle(fontSize: 12))` scales with the text scaler inside Material's fixed 24dp circle.
- **Environment:** web release 360×800, `ur-PK` and `en-PK`, text scale 2. Widget test with the real fonts.
- **Expected:** the digit fits its circle at 200% (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Actual:**
  - At 200% the digit needs 35dp (en) or 50dp (ur, whose inherited Urdu line height is 1.9+) in a 24dp circle. The "2" and "3" are cut in half.
  - In Urdu even 1× needs 25dp.
- **Evidence:** `docs/qa/screens/1.6/gallery_360x800_light_ur-PK_ts2_p18.png` (the "Interests" and "Alerts" circles). `test/qa/design_system_real_fonts_qa_test.dart` "<en|ur>: AppStepper step numbers fit their circles at 200%" fails (`text needs 50.0dp in a 24.0dp circle`).
- **Suggested fix:** give the digit `height: 1` and a fixed Latin family, and either cap its scaling (`textScaler: MediaQuery.textScalerOf(context).clamp(maxScaleFactor: 1.3)`) or grow the circle with `stepIconHeight`/`stepIconWidth` (up to 48) as text scales.

### BUG-4 (S3): `AppStepper` controls overflow at 200%
- **Area / file:** `lib/design_system/widgets/app_stepper.dart:47` passes `controlsBuilder` through. With none, Material's default Continue/Cancel `Row` (`material_ui stepper.dart:643`) is used, and it can't wrap.
- **Steps:** `AppStepper(onStepContinue: …, onStepCancel: …)` at 360dp with text scale 2.
- **Expected:** no overflow; both buttons visible.
- **Actual:** `A RenderFlex overflowed by 27 pixels` (en) / `by 82 pixels` (ur). On the web build the "جاری رکھیں" (Continue) button is cut off at the screen edge.
- **Evidence:** `gallery_360x800_light_ur-PK_ts2_p18.png` ("ی رکھیں"). `design_system_real_fonts_qa_test.dart` "AppStepper Continue/Cancel stay on screen at 200%" fails ×2.
- **Suggested fix:** a default `controlsBuilder` in AppStepper using `Wrap` (or `OverflowBar`) with the localized labels.

### BUG-5 (S3): `AppTabBar` truncates labels at 200%
- **Area / file:** `lib/design_system/widgets/app_tab_bar.dart:36-42`: a fixed (non-scrollable) `TabBar` with `maxLines: 1, overflow: ellipsis`. The height now grows (1.3 BUG-10 is fixed), but the width doesn't.
- **Steps:** three tabs (en "Near me / Online / All city", ur "قریب / آن لائن / پورا شہر") at 360dp and 200%.
- **Expected:** labels readable (WCAG 1.4.4; flutter-ui-ux "nothing is clipped").
- **Actual:** "…All", "…On", "…Ne" in the web build. The real-font test reports truncation for every label in both languages.
- **Evidence:** `gallery_360x800_light_ur-PK_ts2_p16.png`. `design_system_real_fonts_qa_test.dart` "AppTabBar labels are not truncated at 200%" fails ×2.
- **Suggested fix:** switch to `isScrollable: true` with `tabAlignment: TabAlignment.start` when the labels don't fit (measure them in a `LayoutBuilder`), or allow 2 lines and size the height for that.

### BUG-6 (S4): `ExpiryTag` refresh never starts if mounted more than 24 h out
- **Area / file:** `lib/design_system/widgets/deal_labels.dart:126-136`. The one-minute timer is created only when `remaining <= 24h` at mount or at a dependency change. A card left on screen as it crosses 24 h keeps its non-urgent colour and its old text, such as "Ends tomorrow" past midnight. Also, the timer keeps firing after the deal expires.
- **Evidence:** `design_system_qa_test.dart` "a tag mounted 24h+1min out turns urgent once inside 24h" fails (colour stays `onSurfaceVariant`).
- **Suggested fix:** schedule a one-shot `Timer` for the next boundary (urgent start, the 6 h line, midnight or the end), then reschedule.

### BUG-7 (S3): Golden and a11y matrix doesn't meet the gate
- **Area / file:** `test/design_system/widget_gallery_golden_test.dart:37-41` and `widget_gallery_a11y_test.dart:17-21` only run `en light`, `en dark` and `ur light`. The gate says "every widget × theme × direction". **Urdu + dark** is missing, and so are the high-contrast themes the app installs (`app.dart:38-47`).
- **Workaround:** QA's `design_system_qa_test.dart` runs the guideline matchers in ur dark, en light HC and ur dark HC, plus 200% ur dark. All pass, but there are no goldens for them.
- **Suggested fix:** add `ur dark` (and the HC pair) to both scenario lists, then regenerate and review the goldens.

### S4-1: Guard test patterns are easy to sidestep
`test/design_system/design_system_guard_test.dart:10-15`.
- **Patterns it misses:**
  - `\bTabBar\(` misses `TabBar.secondary(`, which has the same fixed-height `Tab`;
  - `\bRefreshIndicator(\.adaptive)?\(` misses `RefreshIndicator.noSpinner(`;
  - `DateRangePickerDialog(` and a `showDateRangePicker` tear-off aren't banned.
- **Comment stripping:** `_code()` removes `//` to the end of the line, so any code after a URL string on the same line is never scanned.
- **Toggle check:** it only looks outside `design_system/`. It never checks that toggles *inside* it are under `AccentPrimary`.
- **Fix:** match `\bTabBar(\.\w+)?\(` (and the same for the others), and strip comments with a Dart-aware scan (or use the analyzer's AST).

### S4-2: Latin text in the Urdu UI takes Nastaliq line height
`ContentText` (`content_text.dart:28-39`) keeps the Urdu theme style, with its height of about 1.9, for LTR content. Latin titles such as "Winter collection, in store and online" get very wide line gaps in Urdu (`gallery_360x800_light_ur-PK_p0.png`, and every 2× shot). **Fix:** when the detected direction is LTR and the UI is Urdu, use the Latin style's height (or `TextHeightBehavior`).

### S4-3: The badge is read as "minus forty percent"
`deal_card.dart:105` puts `dealOfferText` ("-40%" / "⁦-40%⁩") into the summary. **Fix:** a semantics-only string such as "40% off" / "40% رعایت".

### S4-4: Reading copy in Naskh in `MessageView` (R2-8 leftover)
`message_view.dart:47-51` uses `bodyLarge`, which is Naskh in Urdu. Empty and error copy ("بازار آج ٹھنڈا ہے…") is reading text, while `InlineErrorBanner`/`OfflineBanner` (`bodyMedium`) are Nastaliq. The same gallery screen shows both scripts for the same kind of message (`gallery_360x800_light_ur-PK_p6.png`). **Fix:** `bodyMedium` here, or a brand decision recorded in BRAND_GUIDELINES §4.

### S4-5: Gallery gaps
`lib/design_system/gallery/widget_gallery_page.dart`:
- all sample content is Latin, so there's no Urdu brand or offer and the Urdu screenshots never show an Urdu card;
- the Stepper has no callbacks, so only disabled controls are shown;
- `ErrorView` sits in `SizedBox(height: 240)` (`:140`), so at 200% its retry button scrolls out of view;
- the Latin dev labels end with "." on the wrong side in RTL (".Nothing saved yet").

### Urdu font subsetting (`tool/subset_urdu_fonts.py`, `assets/fonts/Noto*`)
- **Source is the old shipped fonts:** the `design/fonts/static/Noto*` sources have the same sha256 as the fonts shipped before (`HEAD~1:assets/fonts`).
- **Shaping is unchanged.** I shaped every Urdu ARB string, plus Urdu digits, `، ۔ · ! ؟ ( ) :` and ZWNJ-style samples, with HarfBuzz against the original and the subset fonts: **368 segments × 4 fonts, 0 differences** in glyph names, clusters, advances or offsets, and no `.notdef`. All 17 tables are kept (GSUB/GPOS/GDEF included). Script: scratchpad `qa/shape_cmp.py`, `python3 -I shape_cmp.py <repo>`.
- **The cmap test** (`tokens_and_fonts_test.dart`) checks that Latin letters, digits and `, . %` are gone. On the web build, Latin inside Urdu renders in Instrument Sans and prices in Bricolage, with **no fonts.gstatic.com fetch**.
- **Note:** no bundled font maps LRI/PDI (U+2066/2069). They're default-ignorable and HarfBuzz/Skia draw nothing, so there's no tofu and no fetch, as seen on the web.
