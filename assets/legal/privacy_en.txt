# Privacy policy
Last updated: 31 October 2026

Sale Lagi Hai! shows deals from across Pakistan. We collect as little as we can, and never sell your data.

# Without an account
- Your language, city, interests, followed brands and categories, saved deals, recent searches and cards stay on this phone.
- When you ask for deals near you, your approximate location is used for that one search. It is never stored or sent with anything else.
- We never ask for card numbers. "My cards" stores only your bank and card type, such as "HBL Visa Credit".

# With an account
- You sign in with your mobile number (a one-time code by SMS) or with Google. We keep your number or email to sign you in, and nothing else from Google.
- Your choices above are kept in your account too, so they follow you to another phone.
- If you turn on alerts, we keep your phone's notification token to send them, and your alert settings: topics, how often, and quiet hours.

# Ads
- The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and sponsor banners that we label as Sponsored.
- To choose and measure ads, Google may use your phone's advertising ID, your internet address and how you interact with ads. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Where the law requires consent, the app asks first, and you can change your answer in Settings, Privacy.
- Sponsor banners use only the city you chose. We never share who you are with sponsors.

# What we never do
- We never sell or rent your data, and never show it to brands.
- We never ask for card numbers, PINs, CVVs or passwords for other services.
- Brands see only totals (for example, how many people saved a deal), never who you are.

# Deleting your data
- Profile, Delete account removes your account and everything kept in it straight away. This phone is cleared too.
- Settings, Privacy lets you clear what is stored on this phone at any time.

# Questions
Write to us at the email address on our Google Play listing, or from Settings, About when this version of the app shows a contact address.
