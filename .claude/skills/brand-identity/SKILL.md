# Brand identity playbook

A brand identity is a system, not a picture. The logo is an "empty vessel" (Bierut) that earns meaning from
the product's behaviour; "a logo derives its meaning from the quality of the thing it symbolizes, not the other
way around" (Rand). So: make it simple, distinctive and appropriate (Haviv's three tests), make it work at 16px,
and make the system around it (color, type, voice) carry most of the personality.

Project assets live in `design/`. The current identity for Sale Lagi Hai! is documented in
`design/BRAND_GUIDELINES.md` and generated by scripts in `design/_src/` (never hand-edit generated files).

## 1. Process (do these in order, write each down)

1. **Brief.** Product, audience, competitors (and their colors), where the mark will live (launcher icon,
   notification, WhatsApp share card, shop-window sticker), languages and scripts, constraints (name may change?).
2. **Research.** Category conventions to avoid, local visual culture worth borrowing (with taste), references
   outside the category. Note what every competitor does so you can do something else.
3. **Strategy before shapes.** Positioning, 3–5 traits, one big idea in one sentence. If the idea cannot be
   said in a sentence, the mark will not say it either.
4. **Diverge.** At least 3 *genuinely different* concepts (different ideas, not three colorways). Typical
   families: custom lettering/wordmark, letterform monogram, symbol from the name or behaviour, abstract.
5. **Build, don't decorate.** Vector paths written deliberately (grid, construction rule, or real type outlines).
   Keep a generator script so geometry is reproducible and tweakable.
6. **Critique with renders, not imagination.** Render PNGs and *look at them* at 16, 24, 48 and 512 px, on light
   and dark, in one-color mono, inside launcher masks. Write the critique down (section 3).
7. **Converge and refine.** Pick one, then fix optical issues: overshoot on points and curves, optical centering,
   stroke weight vs. companion type, small-size variant if needed.
8. **System.** Palette with roles, contrast-checked; type scale per script; spacing/radius/motion tokens;
   voice. Ship tokens as code (JSON + platform file).
9. **Deliver.** Symbol, wordmark(s), lockups, mono versions, icon set, guidelines, a one-page board.
10. **Be honest.** List weaknesses and open decisions for the owner.

## 2. Concept generation prompts (use 3+)

- What does the *name* look like when said out loud? (punctuation, rhythm, a single letter)
- What does the *product do* at its key moment? (alert, open, reveal, share)
- What *local craft or vernacular* does this audience recognise? Use its logic (proportion, tool, construction),
  not its ornaments. Logic transfers; ornament becomes kitsch.
- What is the *tool* that would draw it? (reed pen, brush, stencil, sign painter's chisel tip)
- Can one element do two jobs? (a dot that is also a notification badge, a bar that is also a stroke)

## 3. Critique tests (score each concept 1–5, write one line of evidence each)

| Test | Pass looks like |
|---|---|
| **Idea** | A stranger can be told the idea in one sentence and then sees it. |
| **Simple** (Haviv) | Survives 16px and a 1-second glance. Few parts; no part under ~1/12 of the mark height. |
| **Distinctive / memorable** (Haviv) | Doodle test: someone can redraw it from memory in 10 s. Not confusable with a competitor or a stock icon. |
| **Appropriate** (Haviv) | Fits the audience and category feeling; trustworthy enough for paying businesses. |
| **Mono** | Works in one flat color with no gradient, shadow or second tone. |
| **Scale** | 16px favicon, 24dp notification silhouette, 512px store icon, billboard. |
| **Reverse** | Works light-on-dark without redrawing (or has a defined reverse). |
| **Mask** | Survives circle, squircle and rounded-square masks without touching the edge. |
| **Script** | If bilingual, both scripts look like one family; no faked glyphs. |
| **Longevity** | No trend effects that date it in 2 years. |

Red flags: needs a tagline to make sense; only works in color; meaning depends on a detail invisible at 48px;
the best part is the gradient.

## 4. Anti-AI-look checklist (all must be true)

Generic generator output is recognisable: symmetric blobs, glossy gradients, random sparkles, swooshes that mean
nothing, illegible pseudo-letters, everything "balanced", nothing decided. Avoid it by deciding things.

- [ ] **One idea**, stated in a sentence, visible in the shape. No "it represents growth, connection and innovation".
- [ ] **Flat**: no gradients, glows, bevels, 3D shine, lens flares, glass. (Shadows only as a deliberate flat device.)
- [ ] **No filler motifs**: no stars/sparkles, swooshes, orbit rings, abstract "people", globes, hexagon clusters.
- [ ] **No category clip-art**: no shopping bags, price tags, carts, generic bell-with-dot (unless re-thought).
- [ ] **Deliberate asymmetry or tension** somewhere (a lean, a cut, an off-centre weight). Over-balance reads as machine.
- [ ] **A construction rule** you can explain: a grid, a nib angle, a unit (e.g. the nuqta), real type outlines.
- [ ] **Real letterforms**: lettering is either drawn on purpose or converted from a real, licensed font with proper
      shaping (HarfBuzz for Arabic script). Never hand-fake Urdu/Arabic glyphs.
- [ ] **Consistent details**: one corner treatment, one angle family, one stroke logic across mark, wordmark, icons.
- [ ] **Few parts**: count them. More than 3–4 separate shapes needs a reason.
- [ ] **Survives 16px** in mono. Generators optimise for the 1024px thumbnail; designers optimise for the smallest use.
- [ ] **Culture used with restraint**: borrow a tool, proportion or gesture, not a pile of ornaments.
- [ ] **Not a lookalike**: compare against competitors and the obvious stock icon for the category.

## 5. Android / Play icon specs (details and XML in `references/android-icons.md`)

| Asset | Spec |
|---|---|
| Play Store icon | 512×512 px, 32-bit PNG, sRGB, ≤1024 KB, **full square** (Google applies ~30% corner radius), **no shadow**, no rank/"deal"/"free" text or badges. Full-bleed background; keyline area ≈ 384 px if the logo needs breathing room. |
| Adaptive icon | Foreground + background layers, each 108×108 dp (432 px at xxxhdpi). Outer 18 dp per side reserved for masks and parallax; the **66×66 dp centre is the safe zone** (never clipped); visible window up to 72×72 dp. Logo 48–66 dp. No masks or shadows baked into layers. Vectors preferred. |
| Themed icon | `<monochrome>` layer (Android 13+), same 108 dp canvas and 66 dp safe zone; system tints it, only alpha matters. Android 16 QPR2 auto-themes apps without one, so ship your own. |
| Legacy launcher | 48 dp: 48/72/96/144/192 px (mdpi→xxxhdpi), square-with-radius and round variants. |
| Notification small icon | 24×24 dp, **white silhouette on transparent**; system uses alpha only (colored icons become white squares). Keep artwork in ~20–22 dp. Export 24/36/48/72/96 px. Set the accent with `setColor()`. |
| Splash (Android 12+) | Icon without background: 288×288 dp, content inside a **192 dp circle**. With icon background: 240×240 dp inside a 160 dp circle. Single opaque window background color. Animated (AVD) ≤1000 ms. Branded bottom image (200×80 dp) discouraged. |

Always check the mark's **maximum radius** from centre against the safe-zone radius (33 dp) numerically.

## 6. Color system and contrast (method in `references/color-contrast.md`)

1. Start from roles, not swatches: brand surface, ink/text, interactive, accent, neutrals (~10 steps), semantic
   (success, warning, error, info), plus any product-specific accent (e.g. deal/hot badge).
2. Check competitors' primaries and pick a different hue family for your primary.
3. Light and dark themes are separate role maps over the same palette.
4. **Verify with a script**, never by eye: WCAG 2.x relative luminance, ratio = (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05).
   - Text ≥ **4.5:1** (SC 1.4.3). Large text (≥ 24 px, or ≥ 18.66 px bold) ≥ **3:1**.
   - UI components, focus rings, state indicators and meaningful graphics ≥ **3:1** against adjacent colors (SC 1.4.11).
   - Thresholds are not rounded: 2.999 fails.
5. A bright brand color that fails on white (yellows!) becomes a *fill* with dark content, never text or a thin line.
6. Ship the contrast table with the guidelines and make the generator fail the build on a regression.
   `scripts/contrast.py` here is a standalone checker: `python3 scripts/contrast.py "#1A1433" "#FFC414"`.

## 7. Bilingual typography: Latin + Urdu (details in `references/bilingual-typography.md`)

- Licenses: **OFL or Apache only** (Google Fonts). Bundle TTFs + license files in the app; do not rely on runtime download.
- Pick by job: a distinctive Latin display face (short headlines, prices), a highly legible Latin UI face with
  **tabular figures** (prices must align), an Urdu face.
- **Urdu is Nastaliq by default.** Readers expect it (newspapers, signage). Noto Nastaliq Urdu: authentic, but tall
  (font ascent+descent ≈ 2.5 em), slow to shape, no tabular figures. Noto Naskh Arabic: compact and fast but reads as
  Arabic/"foreign" to many Urdu readers. Use Nastaliq for all reading text; allow Naskh only for tight numeric UI.
- Nastaliq needs **line-height ≈ 1.9–2.2** and +2–4 px over the Latin twin size; test clipping of nuqtas on device;
  use even leading distribution. Give input fields extra height.
- Do not match x-heights across scripts (Arabic script has no x-height). Match **weight and color** instead.
- RTL: mirror layout, not glyphs. "!" and digits are not mirrored. Brand names and prices inside Urdu stay Latin;
  provide font fallbacks both ways.
- Logo lettering in Arabic script: shape with HarfBuzz and convert to outlines; check every dot (nuqta) against a
  browser rendering of the same string.

## 8. Voice

- Voice is constant, tone flexes by moment (celebration, error, empty state, money).
- Write a "We are / We are not" table with 3–4 traits; the "not" column is the guardrail.
- Give real examples per channel (push, empty state, error, store listing) in each language the product ships.
  Code-switching (Roman Urdu + English) is normal for urban Pakistani users; formal Urdu for trust moments.
- Never promise what the business has not confirmed (prices, dates). Deals copy must be checkable.

## 9. Delivery checklist

- [ ] `BRAND_STRATEGY.md`: positioning, traits, big idea, voice + examples.
- [ ] Concepts with renders and written critique (`logo/CONCEPTS.md`).
- [ ] Symbol, wordmark, local-script lockup, horizontal lockup, mono (black/white) versions, SVG + PNG.
- [ ] Icon set per section 5 + a preview sheet (light, dark, themed, masks, small sizes, notification).
- [ ] Tokens JSON + platform file (Flutter: plain Dart, `Color(0xFF…)`, `package:flutter/material.dart` only, lint-clean).
- [ ] Fonts + licenses in repo; type scale per script.
- [ ] Guidelines: clear space, minimum sizes, don'ts, color, type, iconography, imagery, voice.
- [ ] One-page brand board PNG.
- [ ] Generators re-run cleanly; contrast check passes.
